# Project notes

The previous game (Break of Day) was scrapped on 2026-10-13. Its full code (v0.15) is kept on the
`archive/break-of-day` branch. This repo is a clean slate for the next game.

## Versioning (standard for every game here)
- Player-facing version is `v0.NN`, +0.01 per shipped build; `v0.00` means nothing shipped yet. Show it in the game's main menu
  (put the internal build id in a tooltip, not on screen).
- `BUILDS.json` is the build log: version, internal 6-char build id, commit, date, notes.
- Normal builds: `python3 build.py`. When shipping (commit + push): `python3 build.py --ship "short notes"` once, then commit,
  then fill the previous entry's `commit` field on the next ship.
- `version.json` carries the build id (for client auto-reload) and the version.
- The page is served by GitHub Pages from `index.html` at the repo root.

## Current game: Hold the Line (working title) — 1vX tower defense
- `src/game.js`: shared rules + `Sim` (runs on the host's browser at 20 ticks/s). Maps (one lane per defender into a shared base), 24px grid with 2x2 towers (`footFree`), lane headings smoothed and `lane.pace(d, off)` keeps walking speed constant through bends (sim + client), 3s build time; buildable towers are `BUILD_IDS` (Stone Thrower, Pterodactyl Roost, Frost Obelisk, Firebreather, Tesla Coil); Archer Post and Bombard are promotions (`TOWERS[k].promo`, `PROMOTIONS`: `promote` input {id, i}, `to` kind, `need` level, `cost`, `keepLvl`; promotions start the new tower at level 1 and cost `promoCost` = base minus 70% of the level gold paid past the requirement (min 0); Stone Thrower L3+ -> Archer Post (90g; archer levels 80/110/145/190g, 1-5 archers firing every 0.45-0.6s); Pterodactyl Roost L3+ -> Bombard (120g) in the 3rd slot; other slots are locked 'coming soon'). Stone Thrower `stone`: cavemen throw rocks (`throws`; `loose` emits `th` [tower, unit, who, wind, fly] and damage lands after wind-up + flight; volley 1/2/2/2/3, cd 1/1/.8/.8/.8 via `towerCd`, slings from L4; thrown rocks fly 0.35 + d/300 s, slung 0.12 + d/450 s, drawn with an outline). Pterodactyl Roost `ptero` (`bird`): one round trip per shot — `pt` event [tower, unit, x, y, tImp]; boulder lands on a fixed spot where the target will be (lane-predicted, capped at range) after `BIRD.grab` + flight + `BIRD.fall`; splash/fly per level (`towerSplash`, `towerFly`); cd = whole round trip. Tower levels 1-5 (`LEVELS`: Firebreather `flame` 100g cone tower — `towerDps`/`towerCone`, all units in the cone burn, 5s afterburn via `u.bd`/`u.bsrc`, levels add dps/+10% length/+10% width each; Archer Post: 30g, +35/+55/+80/+110, 1-5 archers, staggered volleys, +15% range at L3/4/5; `level` input rebuilds for 3s) and locked promotion slots (`PROMOTIONS`), shared base HP 300/defender, defender per-second income, kill gold split evenly (leaks pay half bounty, `leakBounty`), leak damage = 0.5/gold of the unit's share of its send cost, min 1 (`setLeakRate`; sapper keeps 4 via `fixedDmg`), attacker starts with 30g per lane and earns nothing during setup; income grows only from sending (+0.21% of gold sent, `sendIncome`; Invest was removed; `noIncome` units like the 40g sapper give none), hoarding counter (`atk.hoard`: +1%/s up to 65% whenever the bank is over 60g (`hoardBank`) during open sending, frozen in setup/pause; the AI attacker keeps its bank under that; any spend multiplies it by bank-after/bank-before via `atkSpend`; income x (1 - hoard); shown on the attacker's gold chip), horde units (`swarm`: zombie, skel, gnoll, troop, warband) give x1.5 send income (`swarmIncome`) and half bounty (`swarmBounty`), horde armor (`hordeArmor`: up to 15% less damage for every unit on a lane at 200 units; bar on each lane chip), units move at 95% (`RULES.unitSpeed`), sends open 30s / pause 15s (`sendPhase`, `canSend`; sim flags `noPause`, `gate`, `sendInc`, `hoardTax` (false disables), `spdMul`, `spdStep`, `botMix` (attacker bot saves for each rolled unit) and `botHold()` (bot chooses to hold gold) are for bot tests), level-up rebuild 1.5s (`levelTime`), 10s setup at the start (`RULES.setup`, no sends; unlock minutes and the 20-min window count from its end), full refund for towers sold within 10s of placing that haven't dealt damage (`refundable`), no waves: attacker sends spawn immediately (`spawnAt`, staggered per lane via `laneNext`), 20-min send window then finish-out, 22 attacker units (menu order runner, grunt, footknight first; Ogre and the zombie/skeleton hordes unlock at 2:00) with unlock minutes/packs/armor/resists and traits (`UNITS`; sapper sprints off-lane via `stepSapper` and knocks towers out (`t.off`), knight `charge`, troll `regen`, gnoll `packRage`, necro `raise` from `graves`, mammoth `spill`, shield `frontGuard`, golem/ent/ram damage multipliers), attacker upgrade: unit speed +5%/level x5, new spawns only, defender spells (`SPELLS`: Meteor 125g + Water Jet 100g, 20s cooldowns; units have knockback `kb`/`lv`/`air`, synced via snapshot `mv`, jets via `jt`), AI attacker + AI defenders (any seat can be 'ai'; `LS.aiCol` holds AI defender colours; menu has Defend vs AI and Attack vs AI). Bot defenders counter-build by enemy mix and cast spells only in emergencies (`botCast`: units already past every tower they own on that lane, leak worth 8+ damage, under ~7s from the base; Meteor for 4+ units or a big one, Water Jet only if it can push them back into tower range); `noBotSpells` turns that off for tests.
- `src/tribe.js`: Stone Thrower (`ST`) and Pterodactyl Roost (`PT`) art and animation, generated from the approved demos and inlined by build.py inside client.js's scope (after `const TD`). ST: cavemen idle-inspect rocks/waddle, throw on `th` (release synced to the host's wind-up), L3 bones, L4 stone circle + slings, L5 mammoth skull + non-attacking chieftain + 3 slingers; rocks home on the unit. PT: bird drawn almost entirely in the owner's colour (cream markings, dark crest); roost dead tree (L1-2) -> rock snag (L3) -> crag (L4) -> peak (L5); bird idles (look/preen/stretch), on `pt` grabs a boulder, flies out and releases early (kills use the lighter 'crush' death: thrown a short way and torn, far less than a Bombard 'blast') so the boulder (60% of its momentum) lands on the host's spot at tImp.
