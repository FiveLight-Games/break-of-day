# Project notes

The previous game (Break of Day) was scrapped on 2026-10-13. Its full code (v0.15) is kept on the
`archive/break-of-day` branch. This repo is a clean slate for the next game.

## Versioning (standard for every game here)
- Player-facing version is `v0.NN`, +0.01 per shipped build; `v0.00` means nothing shipped yet. Show it in the game's main menu
  (put the internal build id in a tooltip, not on screen).
- `BUILDS.json` is the build log: version, internal 6-char build id, commit, date, notes.
- Normal builds: `python3 build.py`. When shipping (commit + push): `python3 build.py --ship "short notes"` once, then commit,
  then fill the previous entry's `commit` field on the next ship.
- `version.json` carries the build id (for client auto-reload) and the version.
- The page is served by GitHub Pages from `index.html` at the repo root.

## Current game: Hold the Line (working title) — 1vX tower defense
- `src/game.js`: shared rules + `Sim` (runs on the host's browser at 20 ticks/s). Maps (one lane per defender into a shared base), 24px grid with 2x2 towers (`footFree`), lane headings smoothed and `lane.pace(d, off)` keeps walking speed constant through bends (sim + client), 3s build time; buildable towers are `BUILD_IDS` (Stone Thrower, Pterodactyl Roost, Frost Obelisk, Firebreather, Tesla Coil); Archer Post and Bombard are promotions (`TOWERS[k].promo`, `PROMOTIONS`: `promote` input {id, i}, `to` kind, `need` level, `cost`, `keepLvl`; every promotion needs level 5 and starts the new tower at level 1 for a flat `promoCost` = `q.cost`; Stone Thrower L5 -> Archer Post (130g; archer levels 110/140/175/220g, 1-5 archers, heavy arrows, L1 ~1.3x a L5 Stone Thrower); Pterodactyl Roost L5 -> Bombard (150g, no levels) in the 3rd slot; other slots are locked 'coming soon'; bots promote any L5 tower but rarely get one there). Stone Thrower `stone`: cavemen throw rocks (`throws`; `loose` emits `th` [tower, unit, who, wind, fly] and damage lands after wind-up + flight; volley 1/2/2/2/3, cd 1/1/.8/.8/.8 via `towerCd`, slings from L4; thrown rocks fly 0.35 + d/300 s, slung 0.12 + d/450 s, drawn with an outline). Pterodactyl Roost `ptero` (`bird`): one round trip per shot — `pt` event [tower, unit, x, y, tImp]; boulder lands on a fixed spot where the target will be (lane-predicted, capped at range) after `BIRD.grab` + flight + `BIRD.fall`; splash/fly per level (`towerSplash`, `towerFly`); cd = whole round trip. Tower levels 1-5 (`LEVELS`: Firebreather `flame` 100g cone tower — `towerDps`/`towerCone`, all units in the cone burn, 5s afterburn via `u.bd`/`u.bsrc`, levels add dps/+10% length/+10% width each; Archer Post: 1-5 archers, staggered volleys, +15% range at L3/4/5; sapper keeps 4 via `fixedDmg`), attacker starts with 30g per lane and earns nothing during setup; income grows only from sending (+0.21% of gold sent, `sendIncome`; Invest was removed; `noIncome` units like the 40g sapper give none), hoarding counter (`atk.hoard`: +1%/s up to 65% whenever the bank is over 60g (`hoardBank`) during open sending, frozen in setup/pause; the AI attacker keeps its bank under that; any spend multiplies it by bank-after/bank-before via `atkSpend`; income x (1 - hoard); shown on the attacker's gold chip), horde units (`swarm`: zombie, skel, gnoll, troop, warband) give x1.5 send income (`swarmIncome`) and half bounty (`swarmBounty`), horde armor (`hordeArmor`: up to 15% less damage for every unit on a lane at 200 units; bar on each lane chip), units move at 95% (`RULES.unitSpeed`), sends open 30s / pause 15s (`sendPhase`, `canSend`; sim flags `noPause`, `gate`, `sendInc`, `hoardTax` (false disables), `spdMul`, `spdStep`, `botMix` (attacker bot saves for each rolled unit) and `botHold()` (bot chooses to hold gold) are for bot tests), level-up rebuild 1.5s (`levelTime`), 10s setup at the start (`RULES.setup`, no sends; unlock minutes and the 20-min window count from its end), full refund for towers sold within 10s of placing that haven't dealt damage (`refundable`), no waves: attacker sends spawn immediately (`spawnAt`, staggered per lane via `laneNext`), 20-min send window then finish-out, 22 attacker units (menu order runner, grunt, footknight first; Ogre and the zombie/skeleton hordes unlock at 2:00) with unlock minutes/packs/armor/resists and traits (`UNITS`; sapper sprints off-lane via `stepSapper` and knocks towers out (`t.off`), knight `charge`, troll `regen`, gnoll `packRage`, necro `raise` from `graves`, mammoth `spill`, shield `frontGuard`, golem/ent/ram damage multipliers), attacker upgrade: unit speed +5%/level x5, new spawns only, defender spells (`SPELLS`: Meteor 125g + Water Jet 100g, 20s cooldowns; - Knight Brawl (`src/knights.html`, copied to `knights.html` by build.py; linked from the main menu): separate 2-player co-op mode. Each player is a Sword-in-the-Stone knight (kneels at his stone, any input pulls the sword): left stick/WASD move (a few px of slide after release), right stick swings toward the stick (dynamic stick: the touch point is its centre; swings tilt with up/down aim, torso hinges), Spin button/Space = overhead cleave that flows into a 4-turn light-sword spin (8 pie slices hit as the blade passes, plus a tight always-on core ~4px past his body that hits at most every 0.22s, 6s cooldown after it ends (`SPIN_CD`; move speed while spinning ramps from 0.6x to 0.78x of a run over the first 0.5s); light blade grows as he raises the sword; short gradient trail the blade's length). Dash (dynamic aim stick above the swing stick, any angle, path preview while held, release to go; Shift = where you're moving): tip down + quick squat (0.1s), then the blade tip sweeps 270° round him (low behind -> through the dash direction -> high behind) as he launches ~52px; one slicing ribbon records the blade (`K.slice`) and grows to the end of the dash, 2s cooldown (`DASH_CD`). Specials buffer for ~0.5s and chain out of each other's recovery (dash after its slash, leap after the slam, spin once it has cut a full turn, swing after its hit), with a 0.16s pose crossfade (`chainOut`, `xfade`); moving cancels the dash recovery, and holding the move stick runs straight out of the dash from 80% of its go phase.
